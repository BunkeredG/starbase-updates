Booster 20 (Destroyed) 
Flew on Flight 13

Booster 20 flew on the second Starship V3 test flight and improved on Booster 19's performance by completing the boostback burn following stage separation. Unfortunately, it was unable to relight all 13 engines for its landing burn and, as a result, had a hard splashdown off the coast of Starbase.

Booster 19 (Destroyed) 
Flew on Flight 12 with Ship 39

Booster 19 was the first Starship V3 booster to reach flight (and the first booster to launch from Pad 2). One of its Raptor 3 engines flamed out on ascent but did not affect the trajectory. After hot staging, Booster 19 was pushed the wrong direction, causing its boostback burn to be prematurely shutdown and a hard splashdown in an unplanned location.

Booster 16 (Expended) 
Flew on Flight 10 with Ship 37

Booster 16, while not the final V2 booster or on the last V2 flight, was the last booster created that flew on a Starship V2 flight. Booster 16 performed several tests in-flight, including engine-out capability during landing, before performing a planned hard splashdown off the coast of Starbase.

Ship 39 (Expended) 
Flew on Flight 12 with Booster 19

Ship 39 was the first Starship V3 upper stage and flew on V3's first flight. During ascent, one of its Vaccum-Optimised Raptor 3 engines failed. Despite this, it was able to complete its ascent burn, deploying 22 dummy Starlink V3 satellites (2 of which took photos of Ship 39) before re-entering Earth's atmosphere and splashing down in the Indian Ocean as planned.

Starship Flight 12 (May 22nd, 2026 - 83 days)
